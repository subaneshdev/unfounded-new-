## In-House Ventures

### 1. Ziggers (https://www.ziggers.in & https://unfounded.in/ventures/ziggers)
- **Tagline**: India's On-Demand Gig Workforce & Instant Staffing Platform
- **Category**: Gig Economy & Workforce Operations
- **Description**: Ziggers connects businesses across India with verified catering staff, delivery partners, drivers, and daily wage workers in under 15 minutes with same-day UPI payouts.
- **Coverage**: Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi, Coimbatore, Pune, Madurai.
- **Platform**: Android App on Google Play + Web platform.

### 2. LoopVerse (https://www.loopverse.in/ & https://unfounded.in/ventures/loopverse)
- **Tagline**: The Skill Verification Protocol — Zero Fiction Credentials
- **Category**: AI & Cryptographic Identity
- **Description**: LoopVerse eliminates fake resumes and certificate noise by evaluating real student code, shipped products, and sprint deliverables with AI-validated, cryptographically proven proof-of-work profiles.
- **Platform**: Web protocol connecting verified campus builders directly to venture-backed founders.

### 3. Zachies (https://unfounded.in/zachies)
- **Tagline**: Engineering Circular Bio-Materials
- **Category**: Deep-Tech Bio-Materials Lab
- **Description**: Replaces petroleum-based plastics and structural foams with carbon-negative mycelium composites and regenerative botanical fibres with a 90-day degradation cycle.

### 4. DrinkQ (https://unfounded.in/drinkq)
- **Tagline**: Precision Nootropics & Clean Cognitive Hydration
- **Category**: Functional Wellness
- **Description**: Clinical-grade cognitive hydration formulated for deep-work operators combining Lion's Mane, L-Theanine, and trace cellular electrolytes without caffeine crashes or sugar.
