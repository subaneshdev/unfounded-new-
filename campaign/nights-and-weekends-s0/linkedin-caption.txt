Turn campus talent into working products.

To college principals, faculty leaders and innovation cells across Tamil Nadu:

Unfounded Nights & Weekends S0 is a free online hackathon that helps curious students turn ideas into working projects—and learn how to reach their first audience.

The six-week journey moves through idea discovery, prototyping, public launch, iteration, distribution and a final demo. Students apply what they learn and explain what happened when real people used their work.

Online across Tamil Nadu. No participation fee. Registration opening soon.

The strongest participants earn the opportunity to enter the offline Unfounded Fellowship in Chennai, based on demonstrated ability, consistency and impact.

Follow Unfounded for registration updates. If you lead a college, department or innovation cell, share this opportunity with your student community.

Learn. Build. Sell. Scale.
